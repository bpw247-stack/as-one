As One ("we") is a support-focused social app. This policy explains what we collect, why, who it's shared with, and your choices. **We never sell your data, never show ads, and never track you across other apps or websites.**

## What we collect

**Account information**
- Email address and password (passwords are stored hashed by our hosting provider; we never see them).
- Username, display name and bio.
- Date of birth — used only to confirm you're 18 or older. It is never shown to anyone.

**What you share**
- Posts (including anonymous posts), comments, reactions, photos and song choices.
- Your Corner and buddy connections, requests, and the people you block.
- Reports you file, and appeals.

**Safety records**
- Text that our screening stopped before it was posted (kept so a person can review repeated attempts).
- Moderation decisions about your account.
- For accounts banned for serious violations, we keep the email address after the account is deleted, only to prevent re-registration.

**Device information**
- A push notification token, if you allow notifications.
- Your device's region setting, used on your device only to show the right crisis lines. It is not sent to us.

We don't collect your location. Photos you add have their location and device data removed before they're stored.

## How anonymous posts work

When you post anonymously, your identity is never sent to anyone else's device, and your post is published after a short random delay with its time rounded to the hour. We do store who wrote it, so you can see and delete it, and so a **moderator can act if it's reported**. Moderators are the only people who can ever see who wrote a reported anonymous post.

## Who we share data with

Only the services that run As One, and only what they need:

| Service | What for | What it receives |
|---|---|---|
| **Supabase** | Hosting our database, accounts and photo storage | Everything above |
| **Anthropic** (Claude) | Automatically screening posts, comments, profiles and photos for serious ill intent and signs of crisis | The text and photos being checked, and the post a comment replies to |
| **Expo** | Delivering push notifications | Your push token and the notification text |
| **Apple** (iTunes Search) | Finding songs for song posts | Your search words — not who you are |

We'll disclose information if the law requires it, or if we believe someone is in immediate danger.

## Who can see what you share

- **Corner posts** — the people in your Corner. **Public posts** — anyone on As One. **Anonymous posts** — anyone, with no name attached.
- **Photos** — only you and your Corner, never in the Everyone feed.
- **Reaction counts and your Corner size** — only you.
- **Your name on a reaction** — only if you choose to show it.
- People you block can't see anything you share.

## Your choices

- **Delete your account** any time from the Me tab. It permanently deletes your profile and everything you've shared, including anonymous posts, comments, reactions, photos and connections.
- **Delete** any post or comment you made.
- **Turn off notifications** in your device settings.
- **Hide your name** on reactions from the Me tab.

## Age

As One is only for people 18 and older. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone younger; if we learn we have, we delete it.

## Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Access rules are enforced in the database itself, so the app can't read what you're not allowed to see.
