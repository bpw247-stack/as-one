# As One Community Guidelines

As One is a place to share struggles, goals, wins and life events — and to support each other through them. Nobody supports alone.

## The one rule

**No hate. Ever.** As One has zero tolerance for content or behavior meant to hurt someone.

We remove, and act against accounts for:

- **Hate** — attacking or dehumanizing people for their race, ethnicity, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability, or similar.
- **Threats** — threatening violence or harm against anyone.
- **Encouraging self-harm** — telling or pushing someone to hurt or kill themselves.
- **Harassment** — insults or degradation meant to wound, not to help.
- **Sexual content aimed at someone**, and nudity or sexual content in photos.
- **Doxxing** — sharing someone's private information, or trying to find out who wrote an anonymous post.
- **Graphic images** — gore, graphic injury, or self-harm wounds; hate symbols.

## What's welcome

Real talk. Honesty, tough love between friends, swearing, dark humor about your own life, venting, and frank conversation about addiction, sobriety, mental health and grief. As One trusts adults to talk like adults — especially with the people they chose for their Corner.

## How As One keeps it this way

- **Screening.** Every post, comment, profile and photo is checked automatically before anyone sees it. Only serious ill intent is stopped.
- **Strangers can't write to you.** People outside your Corner can only send kind, preset reactions.
- **You're in control.** Delete any comment on your post, remove anyone from your Corner, or block them entirely.
- **Reports.** Report anything that breaks these rules. Reports are confidential, and a person reviews them.

## Penalties

A person decides every penalty, scaled to how serious it was:

| What happened | What we do |
|---|---|
| Something rude, a first minor offense | Content removed and a warning |
| Harassment, degrading insults, repeated minor offenses | Content removed and a 7-day suspension |
| Hate speech, sexual harassment, unmasking an anonymous poster | 30-day suspension, or a permanent ban if repeated |
| Threats, encouraging self-harm or suicide, doxxing | Permanent ban |

Repeatedly trying to post content that screening stops pauses your ability to comment until a person reviews it. You can appeal any decision once, and a different moderator will review it. Filing reports in bad faith also earns a warning.

## If you're struggling

If you post something that suggests you might be in crisis, As One will show you free, confidential support lines before your post goes up — and your post will only take kind reactions. **Get support** is always on the Me tab. If you're in immediate danger, call your local emergency number.

## You must be 18 or older to use As One.
